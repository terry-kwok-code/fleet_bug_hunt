# Typo in Policies tab banner: "pollicy" instead of "policy"

## Summary
The failing-policy banner on the Policies tab reads "This device is failing 1 **pollicy**" - "pollicy" is misspelled with a double L.

## Environment
- Page: Host Details > Policies tab
- Host: "Fleet's Mac"

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Go to Hosts > open "Fleet's Mac" > Policies tab.
2. Read the banner text at the top of the policy list.

## Expected Result
"This device is failing 1 policy."

## Actual Result
"This device is failing 1 pollicy."

## Evidence
Screenshot attached showing the banner text with the typo.

## Notes / Hypothesis
Straightforward copy typo, consistent across multiple screenshots/attempts - not intermittent.

## Severity/Impact
Low - cosmetic only, but visible and easy to fix.
